Overview

A multi-tenant SaaS platform that turns “no online presence” into a working digital storefront in one sitting — website, marketplace listing and bookings under a single account.

The problem

Getting a small business online meant stitching together four tools — a website builder, a listing site, a booking app and payments — none of which shared data. Setup was slow and there was no single place to run it all.

What I built

One product where presence, marketplace and bookings share the same account. A Next.js front end serves fast, SEO-friendly public pages and the dashboard; a Node.js API sits over two stores chosen on purpose — PostgreSQL for transactional data (accounts, listings, bookings, payments) and MongoDB for flexible content like storefront pages and media.

One account, one dashboard — a business gets a site, a marketplace listing and bookings without touching five different tools.

FAQ

What is MyDigiSense?

MyDigiSense is a SaaS platform that gives businesses a complete digital presence and a services marketplace in one place — a website and profile, service listings, and bookings — so a business can get online and start taking work without stitching together separate tools.

What technology stack powers MyDigiSense?

A Next.js front end for fast, SEO-friendly pages, a Node.js API, PostgreSQL for relational data like accounts, listings and bookings, and MongoDB for flexible content such as profiles and media. This mix keeps structured data safe while staying flexible where the content varies.

Why use both PostgreSQL and MongoDB?

Transactional data — users, orders, bookings — belongs in PostgreSQL where relationships and consistency matter. Rich, evolving content like storefront pages and media metadata fits MongoDB's flexible documents. Using each where it is strongest keeps the platform both reliable and adaptable.
