FlayerMarketing

The website for a digital marketing agency — campaign-led service pages, enquiry capture on every screen, and the performance and markup that get an agency site found.

Overview

FlayerMarketing runs marketing campaigns for its clients. Its own site has to do the same thing it sells: explain a service clearly, prove it works, and make the next step obvious. Every page was built around that sequence rather than around a homepage carousel.

The problem

Campaign work is sold on specifics — a channel, an audience, a result. A site that flattens all of that into one scrolling services list gives a prospect nothing to react to and gives a search engine nothing specific to rank. And a visitor who has to hunt for a contact form after reading is a visitor who leaves instead.

What I built

A site where each service gets its own page with a descriptive URL, a title and description written around the language a client would search, and Service structured data naming the provider. A specific page for a specific query, rather than one page trying to be about everything.

Enquiry capture sits on every page and is tagged with the service it belongs to, so the team knows what the enquiry is about before replying. Validation runs on the server as well as in the browser, and every submission is stored as well as emailed, so nothing depends on an inbox rule surviving.

The whole thing is mobile-first: compressed responsive images, explicit width and height on every image to stop layout shift as the page loads, and lazy loading below the fold. Most campaign traffic arrives on a phone, frequently from an ad — a slow landing page wastes the click that was already paid for.

FAQ

What is FlayerMarketing?

FlayerMarketing is a digital marketing agency running campaigns for its clients. The website at flayermarketing.com presents its services and work, and converts visitors into enquiries.

How is the FlayerMarketing site structured for search?

Each service has a dedicated indexable page with a descriptive URL, a unique title and description written around the terms a client would search, and Service structured data naming the provider. That replaces a single services page competing for every query with one specific page per query. Local business markup and consistent contact details support city-based searches.

How does the site handle enquiries?

An enquiry form appears on every page, not only the contact page, and carries a tag for the service it sits on so the team knows the context before replying. Submissions are validated on the server as well as in the browser, and each one is stored in addition to being emailed, so a lead is never lost if an email fails to deliver.

Why is the site built mobile-first?

Most campaign traffic arrives on a phone, often straight from an ad. The site uses compressed responsive images, explicit width and height on every image to prevent layout shift, and lazy loading below the fold, so the page is usable immediately rather than after a spinner \u2014 a slow landing page wastes a click that was already paid for.
