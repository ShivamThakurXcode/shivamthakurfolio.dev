Motion

Linear only, four moves

No bounce, no spring, no ease. Things move at a steady speed so the viewer tracks the path, not the effect.

Move

translate · 24 frames · linear
chip from one panel to another

Enter

fade + 40px up · 12 frames · linear
captions, new panels

Fade

opacity · 8 frames · linear
console output, scene exits

Draw

scaleX 0→1 · 30 frames · linear
progress bar, connector lines
  • One motion at a time. Start the next move only after the previous one lands.
  • Hold at least 45 frames (1.5 s) after a move so the viewer can read the new state.
  • Something changes every 2 seconds at most, to hold attention in a Short.
  • Progress bar runs the full video, so the viewer knows how long is left.

Instructions

How to make the next video with this theme

Follow these steps in order for every video. They match the preview frame above.

  1. 1

    Write the script around one question

    Start with a question the viewer can answer in their head, such as "What does this print?". Show it in the first 2 seconds. Keep the full script near 150 words for 60 seconds, and record your voiceover after the script is final.

  2. 2

    Split it into four chapters

    Hook (0–4 s), Explain (4–50 s), Answer (50–56 s), Follow (56–60 s). Each chapter gets one segment in the top timeline, and its name appears under the timeline. If Explain runs long, split it into two chapters, but keep five at most.

  3. 3

    Build each scene from the frame layout

    Use only the six components and place them at the y positions in the Frame anatomy table. One caption per scene. Leave the two safe zones empty.

  4. 4

    Animate one thing at a time, linearly

    Use Easing.linear for every move. A move takes 24 frames, an entrance 12 and a fade 8. Hold each new state for at least 45 frames so it can be read. Lime goes on the element that just moved or changed.

  5. 5

    Add subtitles from your voiceover

    After recording, note when each phrase starts and ends. Show up to 6 words at a time and turn each word lime as it is spoken. Most viewers watch Shorts on mute, so subtitles carry the story.

  6. 6

    Brand every frame

    Put your handle bottom-left and "© year handle" bottom-right at y 1463. Use the same handle in the follow button. Type your handle into the preview above to check how long it can be: keep it under 18 characters so it fits on one line.

  7. 7

    Render and check

    Run npx remotion render at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Before posting, check:

    • The question appears within 2 seconds
    • Something changes on screen at least every 2 seconds
    • Only one element is lime at a time
    • Nothing sits in the top 200 px or bottom 350 px
    • Subtitles match the voiceover word for word
    • The timeline segments match the chapter lengths
    • Handle and copyright are visible on every frame
    • The code shown runs and gives the answer you reveal
