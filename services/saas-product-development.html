SaaS product

From idea to a paying MVP — multi-tenant architecture, subscriptions and billing, and the discipline to ship the smallest thing that proves people will pay.

What’s included

Multi-tenant data architecture done right from day one, authentication and team accounts, subscription billing with trials, upgrades and failed-payment handling, an admin panel for you to run it, onboarding flows, and product analytics that tell you what users actually do.

How I work

The most expensive mistake in SaaS is building for twelve months before anyone pays. So we cut scope to the one workflow your first customers will pay for, ship that, and let real usage decide what gets built next — not a roadmap written before launch.

Ship the smallest thing someone will pay for. The roadmap writes itself after that.

Deliverables

FAQ

How long until we can charge customers?

A focused MVP is usually six to ten weeks with billing live from launch. That timeline holds only if we are ruthless about scope — one core workflow done well, not five done partially.

Who owns the code?

You do, completely. Repositories, servers, domains and payment accounts are set up in your name from the start. No lock-in, and nothing that makes hiring your own team later awkward.

Can you keep working on it after launch?

Yes — either continuing feature work, or handing over to your team with the documentation and onboarding to make that clean. Plenty of founders start with the first and move to the second once they hire.
