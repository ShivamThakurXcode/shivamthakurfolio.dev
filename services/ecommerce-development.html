FAQ · E-commerce Development

How much does an e-commerce website cost?

It depends on the platform, the number of products and your payment, shipping and pricing rules. A Shopify store costs much less than a custom headless build; I recommend the right one after a short call.

Shopify or custom — which should I choose?

Shopify is great for most D2C stores and launches fastest. Custom (headless) makes sense for unusual pricing, B2B flows, very large catalogues or maximum performance.

Which payment methods can you add?

Cards, UPI, net banking, wallets and pay-later through Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal or your local gateway — plus cash on delivery if you need it.

Will the store be fast on mobile?

Yes. Images are optimised, pages are server-rendered and cached, and checkout is designed mobile-first, because speed directly affects sales.

Can you migrate my existing store?

Yes — products, customers and order history can be moved from WooCommerce, Magento or another platform, with URL redirects to protect your SEO.

Do you build online stores for businesses in Agra?

Yes. I'm based in Agra and build e-commerce stores for brands in Agra, Uttar Pradesh and across India, and for clients worldwide.
