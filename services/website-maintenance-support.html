FAQ

What does the retainer actually cover?

Updates, backups, monitoring, security patching and a set amount of small change work each month — content edits, image swaps, minor fixes. New pages or features are quoted separately so the monthly fee stays predictable.

Our site was hacked. Can you clean it?

Yes. Malware cleanup, removing whatever was left behind, closing the entry point, then hardening the site so it does not happen again. That is a one-time job, though it is usually what convinces people to start a retainer.

Do you maintain sites you did not build?

Yes, after a short audit to see what state it is in and whether anything urgent needs fixing first. Most inherited sites need a round of catch-up updates and a backup system before the regular retainer makes sense.
