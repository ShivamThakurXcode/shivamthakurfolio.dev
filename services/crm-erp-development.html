CRM & ERP
development

Custom CRM and ERP systems that fit your pipeline, your products and your paperwork — leads, quotations, invoicing and GST, all in one place.

What’s included

Lead capture and pipeline stages, contact and company records, quotations and proposals, invoicing with GST, payment tracking and reminders, and the reports that tell you what is actually closing. ERP-side modules such as purchase, stock and dispatch bolt onto the same system.

How I work

Off-the-shelf CRMs fail because your sales process is not their sales process. So I start by drawing your real pipeline — every stage, every handoff, every field someone actually fills in — and build to that. You get software your team uses instead of abandons.

A CRM your team ignores is a subscription, not a system.

FAQ

Why build a CRM instead of buying one?

Buy one if your process is standard. Build one when your process is the thing that makes you competitive — or when per-user pricing on a team of thirty costs more each year than owning the software outright.

Can it handle GST invoicing and payments?

Yes. GST-compliant invoices, e-invoice and e-way bill workflows where required, payment recording, partial payments, and automatic reminders to customers who are overdue.

Can we start with just the invoicing part?

That is the usual path. We ship the module that hurts most first — often invoicing or lead tracking — get it in daily use, then add pipeline, stock or purchase modules on top of the same system.
