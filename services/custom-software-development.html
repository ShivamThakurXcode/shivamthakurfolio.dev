Custom software
development

Business software built around how you actually work — inventory, billing, HR, operations — instead of bending your process to fit someone else’s product.

What’s included

Requirement mapping, database design, the application itself, role-based access, reporting and exports, and deployment. Typical builds cover inventory, purchase and stock, point of sale, HR and attendance, payroll, or whatever the spreadsheet currently holding your business together does.

How I work

First I sit with how you work today — the spreadsheets, the registers, the WhatsApp groups. Then I build the smallest version that replaces the most painful part, put it in your team’s hands, and grow it module by module from real usage rather than a guessed spec.

The best business software replaces a spreadsheet nobody admits is load-bearing.

Deliverables

A working application your team is trained on, the database and source code, exportable reports, and a deployment they can rely on. Ongoing module additions and support available once you’re running.

FAQ

Can you replace the spreadsheets we run our business on?

That is usually the best place to start. I map what the spreadsheet does, rebuild it as a proper application with a real database, multi-user access and an audit trail, then migrate your existing data across so nothing is lost.

We already have old software that nobody maintains. Can you take it over?

Yes. I audit the existing system first, document what it does, and then either modernize it in place or rebuild the critical parts on a current stack while keeping the old one running until the switch is safe.

How is this priced?

Per module, not per hour. We agree the scope of each module up front — inventory, billing, reporting — with a fixed price and timeline, so you can start small and add modules as the business needs them.
