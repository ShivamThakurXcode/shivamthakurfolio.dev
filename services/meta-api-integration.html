FAQ

Can leads from Facebook ads reach us instantly?

Yes. Lead Ads submissions can be pushed the moment they happen — into your CRM, a Google Sheet, an email, or a WhatsApp message to the salesperson who should be calling. Waiting for the daily CSV export is where most leads go cold.

What is the Conversions API and do I need it?

It sends conversion events from your server to Meta instead of relying on the browser pixel, which iOS restrictions and ad blockers routinely break. If you spend meaningfully on Meta ads, it recovers reporting accuracy and gives the algorithm signal it was missing.

We are stuck in Meta app review. Can you help?

Usually. Rejections are almost always about permission scope, an unclear use-case description, or a screencast that does not show what the reviewer needs. I rework the submission around what they are actually checking for and resubmit.
