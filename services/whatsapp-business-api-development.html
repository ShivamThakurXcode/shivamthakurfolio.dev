FAQ

What is the difference between the WhatsApp Business app and the API?

The free app is one phone, one person. The API runs on Meta’s servers — multiple agents on one number, automated flows, broadcasts to approved template messages, and integration with your CRM or store. It is what you need past a few dozen conversations a day.

Can you get the green tick verification?

I can prepare and submit the application — Business Manager verification, display name, brand evidence. Meta makes the final call and nobody can guarantee it, but a properly prepared application has a far better chance than a rushed one.

What does it cost to run?

Meta charges per conversation, not per message, and the rate depends on the category and country. I set up the account so charges land on your own billing, then build the automation so you are not paying for conversations that never needed to start.
